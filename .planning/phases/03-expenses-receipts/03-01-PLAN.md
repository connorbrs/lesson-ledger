**As a** private instructor, **I want to** attach expenses and receipt photos to sessions, **so that** families see what I paid. (Phase 3 goal from ROADMAP.md; this plan delivers the domain and data foundation: money in cents, the per-family shares, and the complete save, update and delete rules, proven end to end by a headless tracer test.)

<objective>
Fix the architecture of expenses on one thin path before any UI exists: an expense (amount and description) travels from form state through validation into Dexie in the session's single transaction, comes back as "Expenses $12.50" on a Sessions row, and is removed with its session. Then complete the data rules (update diff, photo-capable payload, shares, no orphans) and the pure predicates the UI plans rely on.

Purpose: EXP-01, EXP-04 and EXP-05 at the data layer, and the costly-to-change shape of the expenses rows (cents, explicit per-family shares) that the backup and the Phase 4 summaries inherit. The screens that expose this are plan 03-02 (Log tab), plan 03-03 (Edit Session and split), plan 03-04 (photos) and plan 03-05 (backup).
Output: money and expense domain modules, typed expenses and attachments rows, the transactional write and cascade, form-state support, and tests for every rule.
</objective>
