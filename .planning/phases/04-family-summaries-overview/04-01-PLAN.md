**As a** private instructor, **I want to** send families correct summaries and see my own totals, **so that** billing and my records are right. (Phase 4 goal from ROADMAP.md; this plan delivers the single summary computation and the Turkish message for one family and period, visible on the new Summaries tab.)

<objective>
Fix the architecture of the family summary on one thin path before anything else is built on it: stored sessions and expenses are read in one transaction, turned into one pure model, rendered as the Turkish message from the UI-SPEC example, and shown in a new Family Summary screen reached from a new Summaries tab. Then complete the model's rules (periods, warnings, fingerprint, privacy, ordering, row and screen predicates, file name) with tests, and build the two screens.

Purpose: SUM-01, SUM-02, SUM-03, SUM-06 and SUM-07, plus the half-credit rounding change (D-17) that every later total depends on. The model's shape (credited whole minutes, per-family expense shares, fingerprint) is what the saved message, the sent flag, the PDF and the personal totals inherit.
Output: whole-minute credit rule, Turkish hours formatter, Turkish label module, the summary model with tests, three read-only loaders, the Summaries tab and a read-only Family Summary screen.
</objective>
